QuickBooks Online — DoorFlex invoice automation

When a tech closes an invoice in Dispatch, it syncs to QuickBooks Online (via Dispatch's built-in QBO add-on). QBO then fires a webhook to this app, which fetches the invoice, resolves the parts to the DoorFlex catalog, and deducts stock automatically. Techs never touch a separate app.

What this integration does

  • Auto-deducts stock from the assigned tech's truck for every invoice line item that maps to a DoorFlex part.
  • Tags each deduction to a Job ID (WO#) — jobs-today "Parts Used" column populates automatically.
  • Unmatched line items land in Unmatched Parts for review, exactly like distributor-invoice unmatched rows today.
  • Feeds every existing dashboard tracker: usage-by-tech, usage-by-truck, usage-by-part, top movers, low-stock alerts. No dashboard changes needed.
  • Never touches invoicing or QBO writes — read-only consumer. Dispatch remains the invoice source of truth; QBO remains the accounting source of truth.
